# [PR 51](https://github.com/exoma-ch/brother-printer/pull/51) fix(imaging): make self-laminating band per-tape-width (#50)

## Summary

Fixes #50. The white-strip band added in #41/#47 assumed a single fixed ~9.8 mm (140 px) height for **every** tape width. Hardware testing on a PT-E920BT confirmed the printable white strip **scales with tape width**, so on wider tape (36 mm TZe-SL261) content was confined to far less than the actual strip.

## Changes

- Replace the single `SELF_LAMINATING_BAND_PINS` constant with a per-`TapeWidth` band table (`_SELF_LAMINATING_BAND_PINS`) looked up by `effective_print_pins()`; `self_laminating_band_pins()` now takes the tape width.
- `brother-ptouch-driver info tapes` shows the band per width (a `self-laminating: N px` column) instead of one fixed row.
- Update `docs/vendor/tze-tape-widths.md` and the CHANGELOG (#50, Fixed) to the measured values; update tests.

## Measured band geometry (PT-E920BT)

The band is the **usable** strip height, anchored at the top edge (`pack_raster_lines` row 0). The printer's own ~1 mm unprintable top margin forms the top gap, and the band height leaves a matching ~1 mm at the bottom — no extra offset needed.

| Tape | Band | Strip | White backing |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 24 mm | **120 px** | 8.5 mm | ~10 mm |
| 36 mm | **156 px** | 11 mm | ~13 mm |

Widths without a measured band fall back to the full print area (no self-laminating TZe-SL tape is sold in them).

## Testing

- `303 passed, 10 skipped`; ruff clean.
- Hardware-validated on a PT-E920BT with 24 mm and 36 mm self-laminating cartridges: full-area calibration bars to locate the white strip, then centered strips dialed to the measured heights, then a final real `brother-ptouch-label` print on each width confirming text confines to the strip.

## Notes

- Both 24 mm and 36 mm SL cartridges on hand reported `MediaType.SELF_LAMINATING` (`0x16`), so detection did not need widening for them.
