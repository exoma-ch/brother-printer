# [PR 65](https://github.com/exoma-ch/brother-printer/pull/65) feat(printing): chunk label strips with half-cuts within groups (#56)

## Description

Adds chunked strip printing: a strip of many labels where labels inside a chunk are joined by half-cuts and chunks are separated by full cuts — the batch-printing case from #56 (e.g. 20 peelable labels, grouped in fives).

`encode_strip_job()` previously decided the cut once per strip (`effective_auto_cut = auto_cut and not half_cut`), so half-cut and full auto-cut were mutually exclusive: either half-cuts between every label and no full cut anywhere, or full cuts only. A new `chunk_size` resolves the cut **per page** instead, exposed as `print_strip(chunk_size=N)` and `brother-ptouch-driver print --cut-every N`.

**Hardware finding that shaped the implementation.** The PT-E920BT applies the cut settings of a job's **first** page to every page of that job. Verified on 6 mm laminated tape:

| Job sent | Expected | Actual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 pages, full-cut blocks on pages 2, 4, 5 (page 1 half-cut) | 3 pieces | **1 continuous strip, 4 half-cuts** |
| 5 pages, uniform full-cut blocks (`--cut-every 1`) | 5 separate labels | 5 separate labels ✓ |
| 2 jobs of 2 pages, each half-cut then full-cut at its end | 2 pieces, half-cut inside each | 2 pieces, half-cut inside each ✓ |

So per-page cutting works; switching the cut *kind* mid-job does not. `print_strip()` therefore sends a half-cut chunked strip as **one job per chunk** — each job half-cuts between its own pages and full-cuts as it ends. Full-cut-only chunking stays a single job and lets the printer's own cut-each-N counter group the pages.

This also corrects the issue's "Alternatives Considered", which rejected the per-chunk-job approach on the assumption that a half-cut job's final eject stays a half-cut. It does not — a job's end full-cuts, which is exactly what makes the split work.

## Changes Made

- **`protocol/encoder.py`** — `encode_strip_job(chunk_size=...)`; `_page_cut_settings()` resolves `(auto_cut, half_cut, cut_each_n)` per page and `_page_control_block()` emits it. A page closing a chunk takes `ESC i M 40` + `ESC i A 01` + `ESC i K 08`; interior pages keep the exact half-cut block the encoder already produced. `chunk_size` requires `auto_cut` and refuses to combine with `cut_each_n`
- **`printing.py`** — `print_strip(chunk_size=...)`, validated before USB discovery; `_strip_jobs()` splits a half-cut chunked strip into one job per chunk and documents why, routing full-cut-only chunking through `cut_each_n` in a single job
- **`cli/main.py`** — `--cut-every N` (`IntRange(min=1)`) on `print`, applied to image paths and `--csv`; implies `--strip`, so `--copies 10 --cut-every 5` chunks copies of one image. Kept out of the single-image `print_image()` kwargs, where `chunk_size` has no meaning
- **`tests/protocol/test_encoder.py`** — 9 cases: chunk patterns with and without half-cut, `chunk_size=1`, short final chunk, single-page strip, the three validation errors, plus a byte-equality test that `chunk_size=None` reproduces both existing strip encodings
- **`tests/protocol/golden/strip_job_24mm_5page_chunk2.bin`** — new golden for the five-page, chunk-of-two stream; the existing half-cut goldens are untouched
- **`tests/test_printing.py`** — one job per chunk, short-final-chunk sizing, the `cut_each_n` route, and the validation guard
- **`tests/cli/test_print_command.py`** — `--cut-every` wiring for paths and CSV, the implied-strip route, the `chunk_size=None` default, and rejection of `0`
- **`tests/hardware/test_print.py`** — opt-in case **H3**: five labels in chunks of two on laminated tape
- **`TESTING.md`** — H3 row, the expected three pieces of tape, and a table of what each wrong outcome would mean
- **`README.md`**, **`CHANGELOG.md`** — `--cut-every` example, feature bullet, Unreleased entry

## Changelog Entry

### Added

- **Chunked strip printing: half-cuts within a group, a full cut between groups** ([#56](https://github.com/exoma-ch/brother-printer/issues/56))
  - `encode_strip_job()` decided the cut once for the whole strip (`effective_auto_cut = auto_cut and not half_cut`), so a strip was either half-cut between every label with no full cut anywhere, or full-cut only. Batch-printing many peelable labels therefore had no way to keep labels grouped for handling while separating the groups physically
  - New `chunk_size` on `encode_strip_job()` resolves the cut per page: a page that closes a chunk — every `chunk_size`-th page, and the last one — takes a full cut (`ESC i M 40`, `ESC i A 01`, `ESC i K 08`), and the pages inside a chunk keep the half-cut block the strip encoder already emitted. `print_strip(chunk_size=N)` and `brother-ptouch-driver print --cut-every N` expose it; `--cut-every` implies `--strip`, so `--copies 10 --cut-every 5` chunks copies of a single image
  - **The PT-E920BT applies the cut settings of a job's first page to every page of that job.** Hardware-verified on 6 mm laminated tape: a five-page job whose pages 2, 4 and 5 carried full-cut blocks came out as one continuous strip with four half-cuts, because page 1 was half-cut. The same encoding with uniform full-cut blocks on every page (`--cut-every 1`) cut all five labels apart, so per-page cutting itself works — only switching the *kind* mid-job does not. A job's final eject full-cuts, which the issue's rejected "separate job per chunk" alternative had assumed it would not
  - `print_strip()` therefore sends a half-cut chunked strip as **one job per chunk**: each job half-cuts between its own pages and full-cuts as it ends, which the printer honours. Cost of the split is the head-to-cutter feed at every chunk boundary — inherent to a full cut, not overhead this adds. Full-cut-only chunking (`--cut-every N` without `--half-cut`) stays a single job and lets the printer's own cut-each-N counter group the pages, finally making the long-dead `cut_each_n` parameter reachable
  - `chunk_size` needs `auto_cut` (a chunk boundary *is* an auto-cut) and refuses to combine with `cut_each_n`, which drives full cuts by its own rule. Backward compatible: `chunk_size=None` reproduces both established strip streams byte for byte, pinned by an equality test against the existing encoding and by the unchanged half-cut goldens. A new golden snapshots the five-page, chunk-of-two stream
  - New opt-in hardware case **H3** (`just test-print`, laminated tape) prints a five-label strip in chunks of two; TESTING.md records the expected three pieces of tape and what each wrong outcome would mean

### Manual Testing Details

`prek run --all-files` green (24 hooks); 373 software tests pass.

Hardware, PT-E920BT over USB on 6 mm white laminated TZe:

- **H3 before the job split** — one continuous 5-label strip with four half-cuts, coming free at the end. This is what exposed the per-job latching
- **Probe: `--cut-every 1` equivalent** (5 pages, uniform full-cut blocks) — 5 separate labels, confirming per-page cutting works when the settings are uniform

- **H3 after the job split** (`just test-print -k test_print_chunked_strip`) — passes, and the tape is correct: two 2-label strips, each joined by a half-cut, plus a single fully cut label

Not verified on hardware: `--cut-every N` without `--half-cut` for N > 1 (full cut every N labels, no interior cuts). It rests on `ESC i A`'s documented cut-each-N behavior and on the N=1 probe above; a short follow-up probe would settle it.
